What is CentreConnect?

CentreConnect is an online system made specifically for South African ECD centres. It works on any phone, tablet, or computer — no app download needed. You just open it in your browser and everything is there.

✅

Digital Attendance

Mark children in and out on your phone. No paper register. Records kept automatically. Ready for DSD subsidy checks.

Questions You Will Probably Ask

Do we need WiFi at the centre?
You need an internet connection — WiFi or mobile data. CentreConnect is designed to work well on a standard data connection. The daily features (attendance, pickup) load quickly even on 3G. We will check your connection during the onboarding visit.
Our staff are not great with technology — will they cope?
Yes. If your staff can use WhatsApp, they can use CentreConnect. The daily features are designed to be as simple as tapping a button. We walk every staff member through it personally during your onboarding visit — no one is left behind.
What does "all prices include VAT" mean?
It means the price you see is the price you pay. 15% VAT is already included. So R199/month is your total cost — not R199 plus R29.85 tax. Your invoices will show both the VAT-inclusive and VAT-exclusive amounts so your bookkeeper has what they need.
What if we are already full? Why do we need more applications?
A waiting list is your best friend. When a child leaves, you fill the spot immediately — no scrambling. Your CentreConnect listing also shows parents that your centre is established and in demand. That builds your reputation even when you have no open spots.
What happens to our data if we ever cancel?
Everything belongs to you. All child records, attendance history, compliance documents, and application data can be exported as a file. We give you everything. We do not hold your data or delete it without warning.
Is my children's information safe?
Yes. CentreConnect is built to comply with South Africa's POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act). Each centre's data is completely isolated — no other centre can ever see your records. Pickup codes are unique per session and expire after use. Child safety is our first design principle, not an afterthought.
Will our R199/month ever go up?
No — not for you. As long as you remain a continuous subscriber, your price stays at R199/month permanently. This is a commitment we make in writing. New centres joining from April pay more. You locked in the best rate that will ever exist on CentreConnect.
What if we need help at night or on weekends?
As a founding partner, you have our direct WhatsApp number. Send us a message and we respond as soon as possible — usually within a few hours. You are not going through a helpdesk or a ticket system. Real people. Real responses.
