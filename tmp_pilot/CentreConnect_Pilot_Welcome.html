❓Common Questions

Do we need WiFi at our centre?
You need an internet connection — either WiFi or mobile data. The system is designed to work well on a standard data connection, even in areas with slow speeds. Attendance and pickup verification load quickly even on 3G.
Our staff are not great with technology. Will they cope?
Yes. If your staff can use WhatsApp, they can use CentreConnect. We designed the daily features (attendance, pickup) to be as simple as tapping a button. And we will walk every staff member through it personally during your onboarding visit.
What if we are already full? Why do we need more applications?
A waiting list is gold. When a child leaves, you fill the spot immediately — no scrambling, no word-of-mouth delays. Your CentreConnect listing also shows parents that you are established, trusted, and in demand. That builds your reputation even when you have no open spots.
Is our children's information safe?
Yes. CentreConnect is built to comply with South Africa's POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act). Each centre's data is completely separate — no other centre can see your records. Pickup codes are unique per session and cannot be reused. Child safety is our first design principle, not an afterthought.
What if we want to cancel after the pilot?
You cancel via WhatsApp — one message. No forms, no penalty, no questions asked. We will send you a full export of all your records (children, attendance, compliance, applications) so you take everything with you. We don't hold data hostage.
Will the R299 price ever go up for us?
No. As long as you remain a continuous subscriber, your price is locked at R299/month permanently. This is a commitment we make in writing. From May 2026, new centres pay R499/month — you will always pay R200 less than them, forever.
