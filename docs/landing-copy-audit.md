# Landing page copy audit

## Summary
- Tone shift toward anxious Johannesburg parents: more emotion, less listing.
- Added a dedicated CTA for non-registered parents before the “How CentreConnect helps families” grid.
- Captured the intent of each major section for future iterations.

## Sections audited
1. **Hero** – Reworded headline and subtext to emphasize calm mornings, real-time replies, and the entire application flow.
2. **Parent journey strip** – Each step now mirrors a parent worry: finding trusted centres, avoiding repeated forms, and getting instant replies.
3. **Safety callout** – Focused on reassurance (“only your approved people”), pickup codes, and alerts that let parents relax.
4. **New parent CTA** – Added a frosted-block CTA with two clear actions (register vs. log in) so parents who land on the page know they can still interact without an account.
5. **Family value grid** – Each card now highlights emotional benefits (“keep your parenting days feeling human,” “one profile, every application,” etc.).
6. **FAQ** – Updated answers to speak in plain, empathetic language targeted at stressed parents.
7. **Jobs section** – Added context copy that frames the jobs as trustworthy opportunities for caregivers, keeping the tone consistent with parent support.

## Resulting tone
- Language now directly addresses parents who worry about safety, paperwork, or not being heard.
- The new CTA ensures unauthenticated visitors have a clear path forward before the “How CentreConnect helps families” content.
- The tone stays consistent with the “app-like” feel you’re building across the landing experience.
