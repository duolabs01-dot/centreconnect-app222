# Homepage Audit — 2026-03-12

Target: `app/(journey)/page.tsx` + `app/(journey)/page.client.tsx`

## Are we happy?
**Short answer:** mostly, but not fully.

Current homepage score: **8.0/10**

## What is working
- Strong visual identity and high trust tone.
- Clear CTAs (Directory / Create account / WhatsApp share).
- Good local context (Alexandra emphasis) and social proof blocks.
- Route-level role redirects avoid wrong-home confusion for logged-in users.

## What still hurts conversion
1. Primary CTA language is generic (“Directory”) vs intent-based (“Find a creche near me”).
2. Too many decorative elements before hard proof of outcomes.
3. “Create account” appears early for parents who mostly want to compare first.
4. ECD operator value is not explicit enough above the fold.

## Concrete copy improvements
- Replace button text:
  - `Directory` -> `Find creches near me`
  - `Create account` -> `Save and apply faster`
- Add one hard proof line near hero:
  - “See fees, ages, and next step in under 2 minutes.”
- Add ECD-side CTA in hero strip:
  - “Run your centre on CentreConnect” -> `/for-centres/intro`

## Logic/flow improvements
- Keep browse-first parent flow (already good).
- Push registration ask to after shortlist or first application intent.
- Add “What happens next” mini-steps under hero:
  1) Browse nearby
  2) Compare and shortlist
  3) Apply or contact

## Verdict
Homepage is strong but still can convert better with sharper intent copy and less early cognitive load.
