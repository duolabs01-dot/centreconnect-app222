Chapter 2

Getting Started in 4 Steps

1

Register Your Centre

Go to centerconnect.co.za/for-centres on your phone or computer. Fill in your centre's name, address, contact details, and age groups. Takes about 5 minutes.

📍 Tip: Use your exact street address so parents nearby can find you.
2

Complete Your Profile

Add your fees, operating hours, and a short description of your centre. The more complete your profile, the higher you appear in parent searches.

📸 Tip: A photo of your classroom makes a huge difference to parents choosing a crèche.
3

Receive Your First Application

When a parent applies, you get a notification. You can view the child's details, accept the application, and start a child profile — all from your dashboard.

✅ Tip: Respond within 24 hours. Parents choose the centre that responds fastest.
4

Activate Pickup Codes & Daily Reports

Once a child is enrolled, set up their secure pickup code and start sending daily reports to parents. This is the feature parents love most.

🔐 Tip: Show parents how the pickup code works on Day 1. It builds trust immediately.
